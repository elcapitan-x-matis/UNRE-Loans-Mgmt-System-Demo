Welcome to the UNRE LMS

Staff Loan Management System for the Bursary Department

Papua New Guinea University of Natural Resources and Environment

System Overview

This web application is designed to automate and streamline the management of staff loans within the university. It replaces traditional manual processes with a centralized, secure, and real-time digital platform, ensuring data integrity and enhancing the user experience for both administrators and staff members.

Core Features

Automated Calculations

Instantly calculates loan totals, periodic payments, and penalty rates based on your inputs.

Payment Scheduling

Automatically generates a complete repayment schedule when a loan is released.

Secure Access

Granular role-based permissions ensure users only see what they are authorized to.

Bulk Importing

Save time by importing large batches of loans, payments, or customers via CSV.

Who Has Access?

Super Administrator

Full system control. Manages user accounts, sets granular permissions for staff, and reviews system-wide activity and login logs.

Executive Employee

View-only access. Can review loan data, customer profiles, and generate transaction statements and reports.

Officer Employee (Bursary Staff)

The primary operators. Depending on permissions granted, they can add/edit loans, record payments, manage customers, and process bulk imports.

Customer (Staff Member)

Self-service portal. Can securely log in to view their own loan details, payment schedule, and transaction history.
